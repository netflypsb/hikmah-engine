## Source Material

Original Islamic reference texts (Qur'an, Hadith collections, tafsir works,
scholarly books) are published and maintained on Bayt al-Hikmah — a project
to bring Islamic references into website format for easy consumption, and to
translate original works into as many languages as possible.

Website: https://bayt-al-hikmah-nine.vercel.app/

Note: not all source material is currently available on Bayt al-Hikmah.
New references are added gradually. This wiki references the original works
by title, author, and chapter/verse — refer to Bayt al-Hikmah to read the
full original texts as they become available.
